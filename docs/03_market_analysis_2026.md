# European Banking Market Analysis - H2 2026

*Fictional analysis prepared by AMG Strategy, September 2026.*

## Interest rate environment
After the 2022-2023 tightening cycle, the European Central Bank cut its policy rates during 2024 and 2025. Lower rates compress net interest margins for retail banks, which makes fee-based income (asset management, advisory and payments) strategically more important.

## Competitive landscape
Large US universal banks continue to post record revenues, driven by trading and investment banking. European mid-size groups like AMG compete on regional relationships and wealth management. Payment networks keep the highest net margins in the sector, often above 45%, because of their asset-light model.

## Technology trends
Generative AI is moving from pilots to production in finance functions, mainly for document summarisation, natural-language querying of data warehouses and fraud and anomaly detection. Analysts expect finance teams adopting these tools to cut reporting cycle times by 30-50%. At the same time, investors and regulators increasingly ask about the energy footprint of AI workloads.

## Implications for AMG
1. Grow fee income in Wealth Management to offset margin compression.
2. Benchmark profitability against public peers using SEC and EDGAR filings.
3. Adopt GenAI responsibly, with transparent sources and an energy-efficient architecture.
