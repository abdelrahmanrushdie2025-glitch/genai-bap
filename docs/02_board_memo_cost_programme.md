# Board Memo - Operational Efficiency Programme 2026-2027

*Fictional. From the CFO to the Board of Directors, 12 May 2026.*

## Purpose
This memo asks the Board to approve the Operational Efficiency Programme, targeting EUR 4.5 million of annual run-rate savings by the end of 2027.

## Current situation
FY2025 revenue grew 10.4% to EUR 87.96 million, but operating expenses grew 10.0% to EUR 54.02 million. Finance teams still spend about 60% of their time collecting and reconciling data from 14 separate systems instead of analysing it. Executive reporting packs are compiled by hand in spreadsheets and take nine working days after month end.

## Proposed measures
1. Vendor consolidation in Professional Services (target EUR 1.6 million): reduce from 40 to 15 preferred suppliers and enforce the EUR 10,000 dual-approval policy.
2. Cloud FinOps (target EUR 1.2 million): rightsizing, reserved capacity, automatic shutdown of non-production environments outside business hours, and scheduling batch analytics in low-carbon time windows.
3. GenAI-assisted reporting (target EUR 1.1 million): a conversational analytics platform so controllers and executives can query data in plain English, plus automated anomaly detection on the general ledger.
4. Travel and facilities (target EUR 0.6 million): hybrid-work office consolidation in Paris and Madrid.

## Risks
The main risks are data quality, model hallucination in GenAI answers, and regulatory compliance (EU AI Act, DORA, GDPR). Mitigations: every AI answer shows the underlying SQL or source documents, humans stay in the loop for decisions, and sensitive data stays inside the EU region.
