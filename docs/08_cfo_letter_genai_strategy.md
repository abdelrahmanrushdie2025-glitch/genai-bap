# CFO Letter - Our GenAI Analytics Strategy

*Fictional. Letter to all finance staff, September 2026.*

Dear colleagues,

Our finance function produces reports that leadership depends on, yet most of our time still goes into collecting and reconciling data. The GenAI Business Analytics Platform will change that. From Q4 2026, controllers and executives will be able to ask questions such as "Which business unit grew fastest this year?" in plain English. The platform returns a verified answer together with the SQL query behind it.

The platform also screens every ledger transaction for anomalies and produces explainable alerts for duplicate or split payments. It forecasts revenue and cost trends with confidence ranges, and summarises internal documents, earnings reports and regulatory updates in seconds.

We will adopt GenAI responsibly. Every number can be traced back to its source, humans make the final decisions, sensitive data stays in the EU, and we track the energy footprint of every AI query. Our target is a monthly close in four working days instead of nine, and fewer manual errors in board reporting.

Thank you for helping us build a faster, smarter and more sustainable finance function.

The Chief Financial Officer, Aurora Meridian Group
