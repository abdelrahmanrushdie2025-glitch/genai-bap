# Q2 2026 Earnings Summary - Aurora Meridian Group

*Fictional company. Internal management report prepared by Group Finance, July 2026.*

## Headline results
Aurora Meridian Group (AMG) reported Q2 2026 revenue of EUR 21.74 million, up 3.7% from EUR 20.96 million in Q2 2025. Operating expenses rose faster, by 9.0% to EUR 13.51 million, so the operating margin narrowed to 37.9% from 40.9% a year earlier.

For the first nine months of 2026, revenue reached EUR 67.73 million (+6.5% year on year) while expenses grew 8.6% to EUR 41.61 million.

## Business unit performance
Retail Banking remained the largest contributor with EUR 6.51 million of Q2 revenue, followed by Corporate Banking (EUR 6.18 million) and Wealth Management (EUR 4.96 million). Treasury contributed EUR 2.21 million. Net interest income stayed the single largest revenue category, although margins are under pressure as the ECB continues its rate-cutting cycle.

## Cost drivers
The main cost drivers were Salaries & Benefits, Professional Services and Cloud & Software. Cloud & Software spend is growing at roughly 30% per year as workloads move from the on-premise data centre in Frankfurt to the public cloud. Management flagged that cloud cost growth must be brought below 15% per year through FinOps practices.

## Outlook
Management expects full-year 2026 revenue of around EUR 92 million. Priorities for H2 are cost discipline, the GenAI analytics rollout to shorten the monthly close from 9 to 4 working days, and closing the audit findings on duplicate vendor payments.
