# ESG and Sustainability Report 2025 - Highlights

*Fictional. Sustainability Office, March 2026.*

## Climate targets
AMG committed to a 50% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030 against a 2022 baseline, and to measuring Scope 3 emissions from IT and cloud services. Data-centre electricity was 18% of AMG's operational emissions in 2025.

## Digital sustainability
Wider use of AI raises the energy demand of IT. AMG's policy is "the right model for the right task": routine analytics questions go to small, efficient models; responses are cached; and batch processing runs in renewable-powered cloud regions. The aim is to grow AI usage without a proportional rise in emissions.

## Governance
The Sustainability Committee reviews a quarterly Green IT dashboard. It covers estimated energy use of AI workloads, the share of workloads in low-carbon regions, and cloud cost per business transaction. ESG metrics are linked to executive compensation from 2026.
